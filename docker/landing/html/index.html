A multiplayer space trading & combat game, played in a terminal over SSH. Launch in-browser, no install required.

What is this?

Pilot a spaceship. Trade commodities between star systems. Upgrade your ship and your fleet. Engage in PvP combat, join faction wars, capture territory, pursue storylines, build a reputation. It's a multiplayer persistent universe with real-time news, player-run economies, and emergent politics — all rendered in a text terminal that works from any SSH client or this webpage.

What's in the box

  • Trading — 15 commodities, dynamic prices driven by supply & demand
  • Combat — turn-based tactical combat with escorts and fleets
  • Factions — 6 NPC governments with shifting alliances and wars
  • Territory — capture systems from opposing factions through combat
  • Missions — bounty hunts, cargo runs, escorts, exploration, quests
  • Crafting — mining, manufacturing, research, player-run stations
  • Social — global & system chat, mail, trade routes, marketplace
  • PvP — duels, bounties, faction wars, leaderboards

For the native-terminal faithful

Prefer your own SSH client? The game is reachable via Cloudflare's zero-trust SSH proxy:

brew install cloudflared ssh -o ProxyCommand='cloudflared access ssh --hostname %h' you@ssh.toconnect.me

Or pin it in your ~/.ssh/config:

Host ssh.toconnect.me
  ProxyCommand cloudflared access ssh --hostname %h
